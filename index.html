[ 02 ]— Engineering Capabilities

From concept to product realization.

From concept to product realization, Steelexmachine combines mechanical design, product development and practical manufacturing awareness to create reliable engineering solutions.

SHEET 02 / 11SCALE 1 : 1
01Phase · Discovery

Concept Development

We translate functional requirements into early geometry, exploring multiple directions before committing to a path.

02Phase · CAD

Mechanical Design

Parametric CAD, FEA and tolerance-stack analysis. Every assembly carries documented design intents, not just a geometry file.

03Phase · Build

Product Prototyping

In-house machining and additive prototyping turn drawings into hardware in days. We bench-test geometry against real loads before any tooling investment.

04Phase · Production

Manufacturing Support

DFM reviews, jig and fixture design, supplier qualification. We stay engaged through first production runs.

05Phase · Validate

Testing & Validation

Load benches, vibration tables, salt-fog and endurance cycling. Failures are logged and fed into the next revision.

06Phase · Handover

Technical Documentation

Assembly drawings, BOMs, service manuals and operator instructions delivered with the hardware. Every part traceable to a revision.
