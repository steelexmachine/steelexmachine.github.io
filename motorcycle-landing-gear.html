[ 02 ]— Product Overview

The Motorcycle Landing Gear is a compact mechanical support system developed in-house by Steelexmachine. The concept addresses scenarios where a two-wheeler benefits from additional support beyond what a conventional side-stand or rider footing provides.

The design is being developed with attention to packaging volume, structural integrity, mounting practicality and maintainability. The current scope is exploratory — a working prototype demonstrating the mechanical principle, with the intent of refining the system through targeted application studies.

[ 03 ]— Problem · Solution

The use-case, defined honestly.

We frame the problem before the solution — and frame the solution in terms of what the prototype currently demonstrates, not what it promises.

Problem · 01

Stability gaps in low-speed and stationary use.

Motorcycles can require additional support during low-speed maneuvering, stationary positioning, parking or specific user scenarios where extra stability is needed.

Solution · 01

An engineered mechanical support module.

Steelexmachine's motorcycle landing gear concept provides an engineered support mechanism designed around compact packaging, mechanical reliability and practical usability.
